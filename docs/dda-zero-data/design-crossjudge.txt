All three sketches preserve the nonzero arithmetic expressions and F32 order. [Base’s selector](/home/aivv/.bend/bend2/base.bend:504) accepts affine function types. The guide supports parameter matches, parenthesized lambda bodies, and single-use closures.

Candidate 1 adds two importable signatures and a possible call cost. Its wrappers perform useful adaptation, but helpers admit inconsistent Bool/count pairs. Candidates 2 and 3 avoid that interface growth while potentially paying for captures, environment allocation, disposal, selection, and application. Eager closure construction still occurs before selection. Neither approach has measured superiority.

Laziness Protocol and Minimize Reader Load favor keeping selection inside the existing functions. Type System Discipline favors affine invocation and avoiding independently supplied flags.

Remaining objections apply to every candidate. Checker acceptance, zero-law normalization, emitted branch placement, scalar-count preservation, exact renderer parity, and complete-frame performance remain unverified. Candidate 3’s smaller interface does not establish cheaper execution.

Suggested grafts are Candidate 1’s explicit comparison of helper-call costs against closure costs, and its full-call-path inspection requirement. Keep its helper shape as a separately evaluated fallback. Retain Candidate 3’s byte-identical-output rejection gate and baseline-retention rule.

Inspection only. Read all three complete packages, grounding, rubric, architecture guidance, Base, and `bend guide`. No writes, delegation, compilation, proof checking, benchmarks, GPU jobs, or replay.
